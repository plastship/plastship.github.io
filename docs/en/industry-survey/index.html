Methodology & Background

The plastship industry survey regularly captures assessments from decision-makers in the plastic recycling industry. Results are based on surveys of processors, recyclers, compounders, and trading companies.

  • Challenges

    The biggest industry challenges lie in quality assurance of recyclates, availability of suitable materials, and implementation of regulatory requirements like the PPWR.

  • Opportunities

    Digital platforms, AI-driven quality analysis, and new cooperation models offer great opportunities for a more efficient circular plastics economy.
