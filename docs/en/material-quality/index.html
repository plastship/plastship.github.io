Evaluate and use recyclate quality with confidence

The successful use of recycled plastics depends entirely on their quality. Unlike virgin materials, recyclates originate from different feedstocks and processing routes.

As a result, properties, quality and performance can vary significantly. A structured evaluation is essential to ensure safe and reliable use.

plastship makes material quality transparent – as a solid basis for decisions in sourcing, application and compliance.

Our understanding of quality

Material quality is more than a data sheet.

  • It arises from the interaction of:

  • Material origin and collection

  • Sorting and preparation processes

  • Reprocessing / regranulation

  • Material properties

  • Reproducible performance

  • Processability

  • Ability to meet application requirements

Using material analysis for real applications

The analysis of recycled materials serves not only to collect data, but also to inform practical decision-making.

Structured testing allows materials to be assessed in a targeted manner and approved for specific applications.

Typical applications Our material quality services are used for:

  • General material analyses

    We conduct comprehensive analyses that provide valuable insights into the composition and properties of your recycled plastics.

  • Quality comparisons and testing

    Our tests, such as odour analyses and benchmark testing, allow you to objectively compare your recyclate quality with the market.

  • Batch testing for the production of CoAs

    Through targeted batch testing, we assist you in the creation of Certificates of Analysis (CoAs) to ensure product quality.

  • Data compilation for recyclate quality

    Our structured data compilation helps you gather and analyse relevant information to make informed decisions regarding material usage.

  • Audited suppliers

    Our suppliers maintain continuous contact with us and are regularly audited to ensure compliance with the highest quality standards. Transparency and reliability form the foundation of our partnerships.

  • Preparing for regulatory requirements

    Our benchmarks provide reliable test data to support compliance, approvals and customer requirements.
