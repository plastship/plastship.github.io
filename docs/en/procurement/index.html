Frequently asked questions about the supply of recycled materials

  • The selection of a suitable recycled material is not primarily based on material data sheets, but on the requirements of your application.

    The key factors are processing, product specifications, permissible variations and regulatory requirements. Suitable material streams, grades and supply concepts are defined on this basis

  • The availability of recycled materials depends not only on the market, but above all on access to stable material streams.

    A reliable supply of recycled materials is based on: - secure input streams (e.g. industrial waste, take-back schemes) - long-term partnerships - active supply chain management

  • Typical risks associated with the supply of recycled materials include:

    • fluctuating quality - lack of comparability between materials - unclear origin - short-term supply shortages

    These risks can be significantly reduced through structured procurement, verified material flows and reliable quality data.

  • The quality of recycled materials is determined by a combination of: - defined material flows - tests and analyses - documented material data

    It is only through the structured collection and comparability of this data that a reliable selection for specific applications becomes possible.

  • Material flows form the basis of any recycled material supply.

    Only when the origin, composition and processing of a material flow can be traced is it possible to establish: - consistent quality - reproducible properties - long-term supply strategies.

  • Procurement risks can be reduced by: - selecting materials based on their intended use - establishing stable supply structures - ensuring transparency regarding quality and origin - forming long-term partnerships throughout the value chain

  • Customised recycled materials are appropriate when: - Standard grades do not meet the requirements - Specific properties are required - Stable production processes need to be ensured

    In these cases, the material, formulation and material flow are specifically tailored to the application.

  • Regulatory requirements relate in particular to: - Documentation - Traceability - Material compliance

    A structured supply of recycled materials incorporates these requirements at an early stage into the material selection process and the supply chain.
